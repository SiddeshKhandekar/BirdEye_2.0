# Municipal Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## Document 1: Roads & Asphalt Infrastructure
**Category**: Pothole / Road Damage
**Severity Thresholds**:
- Low: Minor surface crack, no deep erosion.
- High: Depth exceeds 2 inches, causing suspension hazard.
- Critical: Depth exceeds 4 inches on a primary arterial road OR any damage causing accidents.
**Standard Action Plan**:
1. Dispatch road crew with cold-mix asphalt.
2. Divert heavy vehicles via traffic advisory.
3. Schedule post-monsoon permanent repair if temporary cold-mix is used.

## Document 2: Electrical & Civic Maintenance
**Category**: Streetlight Malfunction
**Severity Thresholds**:
- Medium: Single pole outage.
- High: Consecutive pole outages affecting more than 50 meters of visibility.
**Standard Action Plan**:
1. Check junction box for short circuit.
2. Replace LED luminaire head if burned out.
3. Validate grounding wire integrity.

## Document 3: Water Supply Wing
**Category**: Water Pipe Leak / Burst
**Severity Thresholds**:
- High: Continuous leak causing surface pooling.
- Critical: Pressurized burst causing tarmac uplift or flooding.
**Standard Action Plan**:
1. Instant isolation of the zonal pressure valve.
2. Excavate compromised pipe sleeve.
3. Pressure test backfill and relay tarmac covering.
